# Bibliography and literature search

All sources are cited by link; no third-party text or PDF is redistributed in this repository. "Version examined" is the version whose numbering and wording were checked. All web sources were consulted on 2026-10-19.

## 1. Sources

### Primary sources for the problem

**[EM06] E. S. Egge and T. Mansour**, *Bivariate generating functions for involutions restricted by 3412*, Advances in Applied Mathematics 36 (2006), no. 2, 118–137. [doi:10.1016/j.aam.2005.01.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aam.2005.01.005).
Version examined: author manuscript dated January 12, 2005, <https://www.ericegge.net/papers/3412inv2.pdf>. Used: Proposition 2.1 (decomposition, citing Guibert), Definition 3.2 (reduction of prefixes), **Theorem 3.3 (weighted recurrence for an arbitrary direct sum of indecomposable permutations)**, Theorem 3.8 (single layer, Chebyshev form), Definition 3.9 (layered notation), Theorems 3.12–3.13 (two layers), Eq. (18) (three even layers), **Conjecture 3.14** ("For all m ≥ 1 and all l₁, …, l_m ≥ 1, the generating function F_{[l₁,…,l_m]}(x, y) is symmetric in l₁, …, l_m"). The paper reports computer checks for k = 3, a_i ≤ 24 and k = 4, a_i ≤ 20. The printed version was not accessible (paywall); numbering is that of the author manuscript.

**[EM04] E. S. Egge and T. Mansour**, *Involutions restricted by 3412, continued fractions, and Chebyshev polynomials*, preprint, [arXiv:math/0401217v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0401217) (January 18, 2004). Earlier version of [EM06] with the sign instead of the variable y; Conjectures 4.17 and 4.18 (signed versions).

**[Egg04] E. S. Egge**, *Restricted 3412-avoiding involutions, continued fractions, and Chebyshev polynomials*, Advances in Applied Mathematics 33 (2004), no. 3, 451–475. [doi:10.1016/j.aam.2003.09.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aam.2003.09.006).
Version examined: author manuscript dated December 2, 2003, <https://www.ericegge.net/papers/3412inv.pdf>. Used: Proposition 2.9 (decomposition), Corollary 5.6 (recurrence at y = 1), Theorem 6.7 (two layers), Eq. (44) (three even layers), **Conjecture 6.9** (ordinary symmetry). The arXiv version, *Restricted 3412-avoiding involutions: continued fractions, Chebyshev polynomials and enumerations*, [arXiv:math/0307050v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0307050) (July 3, 2003), numbers these as Proposition 2.8, Theorem 6.8 and Conjecture 6.10.

**[Egg07] E. S. Egge**, *Restricted colored permutations and Chebyshev polynomials*, Discrete Mathematics 307 (2007), no. 14, 1792–1800. [doi:10.1016/j.disc.2006.09.027](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2006.09.027).
Version examined: author manuscript dated July 25, 2006, <https://www.ericegge.net/papers/colorcheb.pdf>. Used: Eq. (13), Theorem 3.3 (transfer formula, for every c ≥ 0), Conjectures 3.7–3.10 (Conjecture 3.8 restates [Egg04, Conjecture 6.9]).

**[Gui95] O. Guibert**, *Combinatoire des permutations à motifs exclus en liaison avec mots, cartes planaires et tableaux de Young*, PhD thesis, Université Bordeaux I, 1995. Remark 4.28 (decomposition of 3412-avoiding involutions). Not consulted directly; cited through [EM06, §2].

**[AB15] M. Albert and M. Bouvel**, *A general theory of Wilf-equivalence for Catalan structures*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 22 (2015), no. 4, Paper 4.45. [doi:10.37236/5629](https://doi.org/10.37236/5629); [arXiv:1407.8261](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.8261).
Version examined: arXiv. Used: the bijection between 231-avoiding permutations and arch systems (§2), Definition 7 rule (3) (PabQ ∼ PbaQ for atoms a, b) and Theorem 8 (∼-equivalent arch systems give Wilf-equivalent classes).

### Announcements of 2026 by Burstein, Egge and Elizalde

**[Egg26] E. S. Egge**, *Two old Wilf equivalences related to the Chebyshev polynomials*, talk, AMS Special Session on Permutation Patterns I, Joint Mathematics Meetings, Washington, DC, January 4, 2026. Programme entry <https://meetings.ams.org/math/jmm2026/meetingapp.cgi/Paper/53693>; slides <https://www.ericegge.net/slides/AMS_Perm_Patterns_Jan_2026_slides.pdf>. The abstract and slides present both symmetries (312-avoiding permutations and 3412-avoiding involutions with a layered pattern) as conjectures, checked up to k = 7.

**[BE26] A. Burstein and E. S. Egge**, *Two Wilf-equivalences related to Chebyshev polynomials*, talk, AMS Special Session on Permutation Patterns I, AMS Spring Eastern Sectional Meeting, March 28, 2026, abstract 1215-05-60699, <https://meetings.ams.org/math/spring2026e/meetingapp.cgi/Paper/60699>. The abstract states that the authors prove that both F_{[ℓ₁,…,ℓ_m]} (312-avoiding permutations) and G_{[ℓ₁,…,ℓ_m]} (3412-avoiding involutions, ordinary generating function, i.e. y = 1) are symmetric in ℓ₁, …, ℓ_m — equivalently, the corresponding (involution-)Wilf-equivalences — and that they generalize these equivalences and refine them by the distribution of some permutation statistics. No slides or written proof were available.

**[BEE26] A. Burstein (joint work with E. S. Egge and S. Elizalde)**, *Two Wilf-equivalences related to Chebyshev polynomials*, talk, Permutation Patterns 2026, California State University, Los Angeles, July 20–24, 2026. Abstract in the conference booklet, pp. 25–26, and slides dated July 23, 2026, both linked from <https://sites.google.com/view/permutationpatterns2026>. The abstract recalls Egge's conjecture that both F and G are symmetric, states that the authors prove and generalize *some special cases* of these conjectures and refine the equivalences by some permutation statistics, and states that the total number of occurrences of [ℓ₁,…,ℓ_m] in 312-avoiding permutations and in 3412-avoiding involutions of length n is symmetric in the ℓ_i. The slides list the symmetry of F_{[m₁,…,m_k]} and G_{[m₁,…,m_k]} as "Conjecture (Egge, 2004)", checked for k ≤ 7; present results on special cases and variants (generalizations of the two-layer identity, a class of unary-binary patterns, refinements by descents and ascents, total numbers of occurrences); and propose the conjecture that the generating functions for patterns [m₁,…,m_k] ⊕ (π ⊖ 1) are invariant under permutations of (m₁,…,m_k), for π ∈ Av(312) (permutations) and π ∈ Av(3412) (involutions). The slides do not contain the two-cycle statistic and do not cite [AB15].

**How these sources are reported here.** The March 2026 abstract states a proof of the ordinary layered symmetry; the July 2026 abstract and slides of the same group present it as a conjecture with special cases proved. These public sources disagree, and the scope of what that group has proved — including the announced refinements by statistics — cannot be determined from them. No paper or preprint by these authors on the subject was found as of 2026-10-19 (arXiv, Crossref, OpenAlex, the authors' web pages). Neither the bivariate statement nor the statement with arbitrary indecomposable summands appears in these materials. The proof in this repository was found independently of their work; it may not be the first proof of the ordinary layered case. Clarification of priority is pending.
